usage: fluxsim accuracy [-h] <command> ...

Seal a prediction before its horizon, score it against an observed readout
afterwards, and publish the outcomes-only accuracy record.

examples:
  # seal a prediction first
  fluxsim accuracy register --file prediction.json

options:
  -h, --help  show this help message and exit

commands:
  register    seal a prediction before its horizon
  score       score a registered prediction against a readout
  record      publish one registration's outcomes-only record

docs: https://agenticstiger.github.io/flux/engine/reference/cli/accuracy.html
