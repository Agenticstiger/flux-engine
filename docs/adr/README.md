# Decision records

Each record says what was decided, why, and what else was considered. A record keeps the
text of its date; a later change is written into it as a dated amendment.

- [ADR 0001](./0001-diverge-from-mesa.md): diverge from Mesa for the simulation substrate. Accepted.
- [ADR 0002](./0002-flux-expr-diverges-from-cel.md): flux-expr/1 diverges from CEL. Accepted.
- [ADR 0003](./0003-sdv-bsl-and-aggregate-only-rule.md): calibrate from aggregates only, with no SDV and no fitting to records. Accepted, amended 2026-11-11, 2026-11-16, 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-23.
- [ADR 0004](./0004-concordia-tinytroupe-adapt-the-pattern.md): adapt the patterns of Concordia and TinyTroupe, and depend on neither. Accepted.
- [ADR 0005](./0005-depend-on-flux-spec.md): depend on `flux_spec` rather than reimplement it. Accepted, amended 2026-09-07 and again since.
- [ADR 0006](./0006-input-digest-is-a-closed-set.md): `inputDigest` covers a closed set of files, and the loader refuses anything else. Accepted.
- [ADR 0008](./0008-flux-expr-2-derived-columns-and-rules.md): flux-expr/2 implements FLUID SQL/1 and adds FLUX's extensions, and flux-expr/1 is unchanged. Accepted; generating from rules and checking runs against them have shipped.
- [ADR 0009](./0009-flux-stats-distribution.md): the statistics are a distribution of their own, flux-stats, versioned by its format and API, and required by the engine as a range. Accepted, amended 2026-11-17, 2026-11-18, 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-23.
- [ADR 0010](./0010-faster-generation-through-a-second-kernel.md): faster generation comes from a second kernel, with counter-based streams and basic-operation transforms, rather than from a Rust port of the first. Accepted, amended 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-21.
- [ADR 0011](./0011-rows-follow-their-key-entity-and-parent.md): synthetic rows follow their key, the entity they share and the parent row their key names, through declared rules and statistics that `fluid profile` checks over each relationship. Accepted.
- [ADR 0012](./0012-a-quantile-is-published-as-the-bin-that-holds-it.md): a quantile is published as the bin that holds it, when its declaration names bins. Accepted, amended 2026-11-18.
- [ADR 0013](./0013-flux-expr-3-date-parts-date-arithmetic-and-concatenation.md): flux-expr/3 is flux-expr/2 plus a date part, date arithmetic and string concatenation, as FLUX-side rules only. Accepted, amended 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19.
- [ADR 0014](./0014-init-reads-every-statistic-a-profile-publishes.md): `fluxsim init` reads every statistic a profile publishes, records the ones it does not use, never draws a column as one value unless its profile shows one, and refuses gaps under `--strict`. Accepted.
- [ADR 0015](./0015-one-generation-per-run.md): `plan` stages its execution under the run directory and `apply --run-id` commits it after checking it, so planning and applying cost one generation. Accepted.
- [ADR 0016](./0016-children-per-parent-are-counted.md): children per parent are counted, not drawn: a `counts` entry lays a relationship's child rows out parent by parent, with a minimum, a parent condition and an expected-count hook; parent aggregates hold by construction on it; `allocation: exact` gives small tables their exact shares; a distance tolerance may follow the row count; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0017](./0017-an-entity-is-a-chain-of-versions.md): an entity is a chain of versions: a `history` entry lays entities per parent and versions per entity out on two nested counted layouts, chains each entity's validity from its first start with drawn lengths before a fixed `until`, with one open end, `closeLast` and `finalVersion` end modes and an activation column; tracked columns move along transition matrices, one exact change reason per version, and the others carry over; `previous` generates; an `asOf` entry makes a dated row read the version valid on its date; a longer window rewrites no version; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0018](./0018-module-facts-live-in-the-flux-bundle.md): module facts are declared in the FLUX bundle under the Simulation's extensions, never in the FLUID contract; a profile request tells the profiler what to measure; a fact with a tolerance is bound into `H`. Accepted.
- [ADR 0019](./0019-one-privacy-budget-per-profile-release.md): one privacy budget per profile release, split across the statistics the profile request asks for; rho adds under zero-concentrated DP, epsilon and delta add under basic composition, and the engine refuses a profile whose parts exceed its total. Accepted.
- [ADR 0020](./0020-privacy-grades.md): a run is graded from the profile it pins (P0 assumed, P1 checked, P3 differentially private marginals, P4 a differentially private model and P5 a learned model with no privacy account, both from a generator plugin and added by ADR 0032), never from the bundle's own register, and `EVIDENCE.md` states the grade and the guarantee in NIST SP 800-226's terms; a column's FLUID `sensitivity` sets a minimum grade, refused at `plan` by the governance gate with `FLX-4005` and again by `apply` and `ship`, and a bundle may only raise a floor. Accepted.
- [ADR 0021](./0021-clique-marginals-are-drawn-through-a-junction-tree.md): noisy clique marginals are drawn through a junction tree: the maximum spanning forest of the cliques by the columns they share, rooted at the largest clique, each column drawn given its separator. Accepted.
- [ADR 0022](./0022-rows-read-their-linked-rows.md): a row reads the row its link names, with that row's own rules, so copies (`sharedColumns` and `sharedAttribute` where the Simulation declares `copies: true`, a `derive` over a path) generate; conditional links, sentinels and bridge pairs are module facts under `links` and `bridges`; sibling windows generate over the whole expose; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0023](./0023-a-catalogue-and-the-rows-that-follow-it.md): a reference expose is its profile's published rows; a column may be drawn `given` a linked row's column; a parent may be picked by a weight column; profile format 1-3-0 adds `references`, number and link groupings and per-entity statistics; `fluxsim init` writes a catalogue and draws a stable trait from its per-entity statistic. Accepted.
- [ADR 0024](./0024-dates-follow-their-parent-rows-lifetime.md): a row may hold a lifetime, a date may be declared within the lifetime of the row its link names, through a chain of links too, and drawn there as an offset from its start, cut at its end and at the window; an end is drawn as a duration and stays open past the window; a measured share of rows violates each fact, and `fluxsim verify` scores every level; hidden columns and tables are drawn and never written; profile format 1-3-0 gains date pairs and request format 1-1-0 asks for them; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0025](./0025-dates-follow-seasonal-rates-dwell-times-and-exposure.md): a date the dates stream sets may follow seasonal rates by month, day of week and hour of week, drawn by thinning with 128 proposals at fixed positions and a fixed fallback; a lifetime's duration may be a dwell family given a state, a histogram or an exponential per state; a parent's number of children may follow its exposure, its active time inside the window weighted by the rates, through the expected-count hook; profile format 1-3-0 gains calendars and exposure, and request format 1-1-0 asks for them; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0027](./0027-a-history-projects-snapshots-events-and-periods.md): a history projects a snapshot (each entity's last version), change events (Debezium's `c`, `u` and `d` with before and after columns, recorded after a lag) and periodic rows (one per held version, period and kind, periods laid from the window's start, exact counts per period); a projected emit's rows are derived at plan time; a longer window appends periods and rewrites no row; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0028](./0028-consumer-queries-run-locked-compile-requirements-and-calibrate.md): consumer queries are declared under `consumers`, checked and rewritten with sqlglot, and counted in a locked DuckDB in a process of their own; their literals, patterns and sentinels become requirements; the profile request asks for their rates and hands over a column workload; `verify` reports calibration and held-out rates apart; a fit moves only assumed values into a calibration patch, and a bundle is called calibrated only when held-out error holds; `conditions` fix values or seed rows, drawn exactly through the junction tree. Accepted.
- [ADR 0029](./0029-response-families-forecast-the-next-state-and-every-forecast-is-scored.md): a response family estimates a treatment's effect by segment from the pinned state's grouped query rates and applies it to its journey's reactions; forecasts for the next state run the sweep and are sealed in the outcome-accuracy store before that state is seen; the next state scores every forecast with its interval score, drift between the two states flags a stale calibration, and the evidence pack lists every score. Accepted.
- [ADR 0030](./0030-identifier-formats-text-cells-and-profile-overlays.md): a column follows its FLUID `identifierFormat` under `identifiers`, unique by construction through `permute/2`, with pattern shares or a prefix from the linked row; a text column is drawn from Faker under `text`, pinned exactly, on a stream of its own through the engine's own random source; a named RFC 7396 merge patch over the pinned profile makes one bundle per overlay, and the register names each overlay's numbers and the statistic that would replace each assumption. Overlays (sections 7 and 8) are accepted and shipped in 0.10.0; identifiers and text cells (sections 1 to 6) are proposed and not built.
- [ADR 0031](./0031-the-report-the-story-and-publishing.md): `EVIDENCE.md` draws the population against its targets as Mermaid `xychart-beta` text and shows consumer rates before and after calibration; `fluxsim story` prints one entity's linked rows in time order from attested bytes; the `[publish]` extra adds object storage, Iceberg and Delta sinks whose credentials come only from the environment or the platform's own chain. Accepted, shipped in 0.10.0.
- [ADR 0032](./0032-a-generator-plugin-is-declared-graded-and-stored.md): a generator plugin loads only from a pinned `flux_engine.generators` entry point whose code lies in an installed distribution, after the governance gate passes its declared grade and the person names it with `--allow-generator`; a plugin states only `P4`, a differentially private model whose account and model digest the bundle declares, or `P5`, the weakest pattern, a learned model with no such account; a plugin's bytes are staged and committed, and regenerated only where its record says they reproduce. Accepted, shipped in 0.10.0.
- [ADR 0033](./0033-agent-skills-drive-fluxsim-and-a-check-keeps-them-in-step.md): eight agent skills in the open Agent Skills format drive the `fluxsim` command line; one Claude Code plugin, rooted at `skills/` and listed by a marketplace at the repository root, installs them, and Codex and Cursor read the same folders; `scripts/check_skills.py`, in `make check`, holds every command, option, FLX code, page and declaration a skill names to the engine, and refuses what a skill must never tell an agent to do. Accepted.
- [ADR 0035](./0035-number-columns-are-drawn-through-a-gaussian-copula-of-published-rank-correlations.md): number columns are drawn together through a Gaussian copula of the rank correlations a profile publishes (`kendallTau/1` and `spearmanRho/1`, exact or with noise charged `4 / (f + 1)` and `9 / (f + 1)` under a public row floor), made valid at plan time by shrinking towards the identity in pinned decimal arithmetic and drawn on the new `copula` seed path with the place inside a bin taken from the copula's own residual; a profile states how its cliques were selected (`cliqueSelection`) and what the selection spent; kernel 0.2.0 only. Accepted.
- [ADR 0036](./0036-binned-number-columns-join-clique-marginals.md): a clique may hold number columns by their bins beside categorical columns (`cliqueMarginal/2`), a column may be drawn given the bin of another, a statistic may be grouped by the bins of a number column (`groupByBins`), and the place inside a bin is drawn as a histogram draws it; no seed path is added. Accepted.
- [ADR 0037](./0037-child-columns-and-child-counts-follow-their-parent-and-a-unit-can-follow-its-children.md): a parent's number of children is drawn given one of its columns on the counted layout (`counts[].given`), relationship statistics may be grouped by a parent column, a unit of privacy may follow its child rows through relationships capped by a public `maxChildren`, and a noisy statistic of another table is charged the rows one unit contributes there; no seed path is added. Accepted.
